Premium $19.99 single-player rhythm-action roguelike. Chinese mythology setting.

**Why:** Indie/AI-assisted 4-person team. 12+ month full-vision scope.

**How to apply:** All economy design must serve a premium, no-MTX model. Session length target is 20-30 min/run, 60-90 min/day. Scope gates are hard — MVP is 6-8 weeks.

## Economy Facts

- Meta-currency: 功勋 (Merit) — permanent, retained on death
- Meta-progression: multiplier upgrades (HP / Attack / Dash CD) + weapon unlocks
- In-run: 3-choice perk selection (PerkSO), rarity-tiered
- Perk design priority: rules-modifying > stat boosts (Pillar 4)
- MVP economy: 1 meta-upgrade option, 3-5 perks (at least 1 rules-modifying)
- Full Vision: 3 weapons x complete combo trees; 50+ perks

## Scope Tiers

| Tier | Timeline | Economy State |
|------|----------|---------------|
| MVP | M0-M2 ~6-8 wks | 1 weapon, 1 upgrade option, 5 perks |
| Vertical Slice | M0-M4 ~4-5 mo | 2 weapons, meta-progression, 10+ perks |
| Alpha | M0-M5 ~7-9 mo | 3 weapons, 2 bosses, 30+ perks |
| Full Vision | 12+ mo | Full content, 50+ perks |

## Game Pillars (Economy-Relevant)

- Pillar 1 "Rhythm IS Combat": no bypass of rhythm precision — permanent upgrades MUST NOT trivialize precision
- Pillar 4 "Build Depth Over Stat Inflation": rules-modifying perks preferred over stat boosts
